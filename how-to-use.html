WELCOME TO DBH

How to use the Agent Portal

This guide explains account creation, email verification, sign-in, onboarding, admin review, property work, notifications and withdrawals. Follow the steps in order and use only your own contact and banking details.

1. Create your agent account

Use the official DBH agent registration page.

1
Open Create an agent account

Enter your full name, email address, phone number and WhatsApp number. Use an email account you can open right now.

2
Confirm your information

Check that your phone and WhatsApp numbers are correct, then accept the declaration confirming that the details are yours and accurate.

3
Request the email code

Tap “Send verification code”. Check your inbox and spam/junk folder. If it does not arrive, use the resend option on the page.

4
Verify your email

Enter the six-digit code and tap “Verify email”. When verification succeeds, DBH takes you to the agent onboarding area.

If the page says the email is already registered, do not create another account with a different spelling. Go to sign in and continue with that registered email.
Open registration

2. Sign in to your account

The current agent login uses an email verification code, not a password.

1
Enter your registered email

Open the sign-in page and enter the same email address used to create your DBH agent account.

2
Send and enter the code

Tap “Send sign-in code”, check your email and enter the six-digit code. You can request a new code if needed.

3
Verify and continue

Tap “Verify & continue”. DBH checks the account and redirects you to your saved agent onboarding progress.

Never give your email code to another person. If you cannot access your registered email, contact DBH support.
Open sign in

3. Complete agent onboarding

Registration and full agent activation are separate steps.

1
Complete your agent profile

Follow the prompts in the onboarding pages and check your contact details carefully.

2
Upload the requested documents

Provide the legal/identity documents requested by the page. Upload clear, valid documents belonging to you and make sure the details are readable.

3
Complete the registration payment step

Read the fee, currency conversion and payment instructions shown in the official DBH onboarding flow before paying. Keep your payment reference and receipt.

4
Return to check your progress

Your saved application can be continued after signing in again. Follow the status and next-action instructions shown in your account.

Do not pay a person’s private account or send money because someone messages you. Use only the payment method and amount shown inside the official DBH page, and keep the receipt.
Continue onboarding Agent documents

4. Verification and approval

Creating an account does not mean the agent is already approved.

DBH administrators review the application, supplied documents and required verification steps. If DBH asks you to confirm your WhatsApp number or correct a document, follow the request through official DBH channels. Wait for your application status to change before assuming all agent features are unlocked.

If your application is pending, check notifications and your onboarding page for updates rather than submitting duplicate applications.

5. Use the dashboard

Your dashboard is the starting point after sign-in.

Review your application status, account notices, wallet summary and available tools. Some features may remain locked until the required registration, document and admin approval steps are complete. Use the navigation links to open each feature, and use the dashboard link to return.

6. Property listings

Publish accurate listings for customers to discover.

1
Open the property/listing tool

Use the property management link available in your agent dashboard once it is unlocked.

2
Add complete property details

Enter the correct title, location, price, description and contact information. Upload a clear front image and at least two additional useful images where requested; add more images if the form allows it.

3
Submit for review

Check spelling, prices, ownership/authorization and images before submission. Only listings approved by DBH should be expected to appear publicly to customers.

Never advertise a property you are not authorized to represent, or misstate its price, location, documents or availability.

7. Read notifications

Use notifications to keep track of account activity.

1
Open Notifications

Check for messages about application review, documents, payments, listings and account status.

2
Read and follow links

Open “Open related page” when provided. Use “Mark read” on one notification or “Mark all as read” when finished.

3
Refresh or filter

Tap Refresh to load the latest updates, or choose Unread only to focus on messages you have not read.

Open notifications

8. Wallet and withdrawals

Check your balance and understand the approval process before requesting a payout.

1
Review your available balance

Open Wallet and check available funds, pending/reserved amounts and transaction history. A pending amount may not be available for a new request.

2
Set your withdrawal PIN

Create a six-digit withdrawal PIN if prompted. Do not use your bank-card PIN, and never share the withdrawal PIN with staff.

3
Select and verify your bank

Choose your bank, enter the ten-digit account number and fetch the account holder name. Check the name carefully before continuing.

4
Check deductions and net amount

Enter the withdrawal amount and review the fee preview, tax deduction and estimated amount to receive before submitting.

5
Verify email and submit

Request the email code, enter it and press Verify code. Then enter your withdrawal PIN, confirm the details and submit the request.

6
Wait for admin review

DBH Admin approval is required before a bank transfer is sent. Track the request in withdrawal history and contact support if the status needs clarification.

The withdrawal page currently shows a minimum of ₦1,000 and maximum of ₦5,000,000 per request. Review the fee preview every time; do not rely on an old screenshot or message for the final net amount.

9. Keep your account safe

Protect your identity, email and money.

Protect codes and PINs

Do not share email codes, withdrawal PINs or sign-in sessions. DBH staff should not ask you to send these secrets in chat.

Protect identity documents

Submit sensitive identity details only through the official secure DBH flow when requested. Do not put BVN or NIN in ordinary messages, listings or support chats.

Use your own device when possible

Sign out on shared devices and avoid saving codes or sensitive details in public browsers.

10. Common questions

Quick answers to common agent portal issues.

I did not receive my email code. What should I do?

Check the inbox, spam and junk folders, confirm the email address, then use the resend option. If it still does not arrive, wait briefly and contact DBH support.

Why can’t I access all dashboard features?

Some features depend on completed onboarding, required payment, document review and admin approval. Check your application status and notifications.

My withdrawal is pending. Has the money been sent?

Not necessarily. A pending request may be awaiting DBH Admin review. Check the withdrawal history status and wait for a confirmed transfer status.

Can I use another person’s bank account?

Use an account only when it is permitted by DBH’s current payout rules and the account holder details are correct. Always verify the displayed account name and do not submit false information.

Where do I find new updates?

Open Notifications from the dashboard or the button at the top of this guide. Refresh the page to load the latest available updates.
