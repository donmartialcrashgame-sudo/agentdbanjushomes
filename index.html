Frequently asked questions

Does payment automatically approve me?

No. Admin approval happens first. Payment is the activation step after an application has been approved.

Can Admin change the registration fee?

Yes. The fee will be stored as a DBH setting so Admin can change it without editing this page.

When can I upload properties?

After approval and successful verification of the required registration payment.

Will customers see my property immediately?

Agent properties can remain pending until DBH reviews and approves them for publication.
